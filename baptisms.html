What we believe

Baptism by immersion

We believe that Baptism is the immersion of a believer in water by a proper administrator (an ordained elder) in the name of the Father, Son, and the Holy Ghost.

Matt. 28:19; Mark 16:12–16; Acts 8:36, 39; Rom. 6:3–4; Col. 2:12 · Read all our Articles of Faith

Thinking about baptism?

Talk with Pastor Miller or any of our deacons after service. Membership at Flint River is by profession of faith in Jesus Christ and baptism by immersion.
